Project Aegis – Achievement System

Version: 1.0

1. Purpose

The Achievement System celebrates meaningful milestones throughout the athlete's journey.

Objectives:

Reward consistency
Encourage healthy habits
Reinforce recovery
Recognise progress
Increase long-term engagement

Achievements should never encourage training through pain or skipping recovery.

2. Achievement Principles

Every achievement must:

Promote safe training.
Be attainable.
Encourage long-term habits.
Avoid rewarding excessive volume.
Celebrate improvement rather than perfection.

5. Getting Started Achievements
   Welcome Aboard

Unlock:

Complete onboarding.

Coach Message:

"Your journey starts today."

First Recovery Check

Unlock:

Complete the first recovery assessment.

Coach Message:

"Understanding how you feel is the first step toward smarter training."

First Workout

Unlock:

Complete one workout.

Coach Message:

"Excellent start. One workout completed, many more to come."

6. Consistency Achievements
   One Week Strong

Unlock:

7 consecutive days with a completed workout or prescribed recovery session.

Two Weeks Strong

Unlock:

14 consecutive days.

One Month Strong

Unlock:

30 consecutive days.

Century Streak

Unlock:

100 consecutive days.

Streaks should allow designated recovery days prescribed by the app without breaking progress.

Recovery Master

Unlock:

Sleep Hero

Unlock:

Average at least 7.5 hours of sleep over 14 consecutive recovery logs.

13. Hidden Achievements

Optional achievements revealed only after unlocking.

Examples:

Early Bird

Complete 20 workouts before 7:00 AM.

Night Owl

Complete 20 evening recovery sessions.

Never Give Up

Resume and finish 10 interrupted workouts.

14. Progress Tracking

Locked achievements should display progress.

Example:

Strength Builder

18 / 25 Workouts Completed

Users should always know how close they are to unlocking the next milestone.

15. Badge Design

Each achievement contains:

Badge icon
Badge colour
Tier
Title
Description
Unlock date

Locked badges should appear muted.

16. Coach Messages

Examples:

Bronze

"Great start. Keep building your routine."

Silver

"Your consistency is becoming a habit."

Gold

"Outstanding commitment. You're making steady progress."

Platinum

"An exceptional milestone. Your long-term dedication is paying off."

17. Celebration Behaviour

When unlocked:

Small animation
Haptic feedback
Achievement card
Optional notification
Save to achievement history

Celebrations should be brief and not interrupt the workout.

18. Reward Philosophy

Achievements provide:

Recognition
Motivation
Positive reinforcement

They do not provide:

Competitive rankings
Performance advantages
Pressure to overtrain

19. Accessibility

Achievement cards should:

Support screen readers
Use icons plus text
Avoid relying solely on colour
Display clear descriptions

20. Future Expansion

Future versions may include:

Seasonal challenges
Coach-designed programmes
Community events (if multiplayer is introduced)
Charity milestones
Wearable-based achievements

21. Technical Rules

Achievements should:

Unlock automatically.
Never duplicate.
Be timestamped.
Persist after app updates.
Sync safely if cloud backup is added in the future.

22. Testing Checklist

Verify:

Unlock conditions
Progress calculations
Badge display
Notifications
Duplicate prevention
Data persistence
Accessibility labels

23. Success Criteria

The Achievement System is successful when it:

Encourages healthy habits.
Rewards consistency over intensity.
Makes progress feel meaningful.
Supports long-term engagement without encouraging unsafe behaviour.
